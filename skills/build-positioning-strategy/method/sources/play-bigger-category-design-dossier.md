## Method contribution

The category-design contribution is a burden-of-proof reversal. A team does
not earn a new market frame by proving that existing categories are crowded.
It earns a category-creation *hypothesis* by showing that an existing frame
obscures a material buyer problem or the offer's solution logic, that buyers
can recognize or learn the new point of view, and that the company can carry
the point of view consistently through its product, proof, sales, and demand
work.

For KB-3 this becomes an explicit four-part assessment:

1. **Existing-frame comprehension:** does a known buyer use or understand the
   candidate frame, and does it activate the right comparison?
2. **Value fidelity:** does the frame make the established or hypothesis-level
   KB-2 value clearer, without promising capabilities, buyer type, or price
   logic the offer cannot meet?
3. **New-frame warrant:** what buyer problem is hidden by the existing frame,
   what different solution logic solves it, and which buyer language supports
   that diagnosis?
4. **Conditioning capacity:** what repeatable product, evidence, sales, and
   market activities will teach the frame, who owns them, and what time/risk is
   accepted?

If the first two are supported, KB-3 can select an existing frame. If the
first two fail but the last two are evidenced, it can return a
`category_creation_hypothesis`. If the buyer point of view or capacity is
unknown, it returns `research_needed`. If the requested decision rests on
novelty, founder preference, or category crowding, it is `blocked`.

## Worked reasoning

Suppose customer evidence shows a services team calls its current problem
"coordination between systems." An existing coordination frame makes an
observed spreadsheet alternative and a synchronization capability easy to
compare. It may be the prudent frame even if incumbent products exist. A
founder proposes "operational truth layer" to escape competition. The new term
does not yet identify an evidenced buyer problem distinct from coordination,
and no team can show how it will teach the idea beyond a website headline. The
proper output is not a rejection of all category creation. It is a blocked
claim: the proposed label is not a strategic program.

Contrast a case where repeated customer interviews show that the coordination
frame causes buyers to expect task management, while their actual struggle is
reconciling authoritative operational state across systems. If a coherent
buyer group uses language for that struggle, the product has distinct evidence
for its solution logic, and the company has a committed proof, sales, product,
and education program, category creation becomes a researchable hypothesis.
It is still not a current market fact.

## Counterexamples and limits

**Crowding escape:** "The category is full, so we need a new one" fails because
it says nothing about buyer understanding, value fidelity, or capability to
condition a market.

**Terminology theatre:** a slogan and visual identity are not a problem point
of view, a product design, or a customer learning system.

**Frame determinism:** even a clear category frame does not prove a buyer will
switch, value the outcome, or perceive the offer as intended. KB-1, KB-2,
KB-5, and KB-6 retain those decisions.
