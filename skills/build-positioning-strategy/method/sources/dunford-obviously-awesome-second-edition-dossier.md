# Dunford Obviously Awesome Second Edition: Bounded Claim Dossier

This dossier paraphrases method claims from the book in a few sentences each. It is not a summary or substitute for the book; read the book for the full method. It extends the positioning domain without changing the tested V2 Dunford dossier. The source can supply method logic; it cannot supply facts about a company or prove that a real positioning choice is correct.

| Claim ID | Pages | Bounded claim | Permitted decision use | Residual limit |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| OA2-01 | 59-78 | Before a positioning exercise, confirm product readiness, customer-facing purpose, positioning object, segment scope, champion persona, and a cross-functional decision team. | V3 readiness and scope admission gate. | The preflight checks are a minimum, not a guarantee that the exercise will succeed. |
| OA2-02 | 46-58, 91 | The positioning components form a dependency chain from alternatives through distinct capabilities and differentiated value to best-fit accounts and market category. | V3 routing order and upstream-state preservation. | Use the chain as an ordering rule, not as evidence for any link. |
| OA2-03 | 48, 98-102 | A distinct capability is something the product or company has that the real alternatives do not; capture the broad set before deciding importance, and keep capability separate from customer value. | Produce a distinct-capability record before KB-2 consumes it. | Distinctness still requires company and alternative evidence. The source does not prove any capability is distinct. |
| OA2-04 | 50-53, 111-120 | Best-fit accounts care acutely about the differentiated value, can buy, and can be reached; characteristics must explain why the value matters rather than merely describe a broad firmographic class. | Select a value-linked best-fit customer/account hypothesis after KB-2. | Best-fit does not prove a Moore-style beachhead or adoption readiness. |
| OA2-05 | 121-135 | Market choice has three strategic styles: compete inside an established category, narrow to a subsegment where the offer can lead, or hypothesize a new game when existing categories hide the value. New-category work has the highest education and execution burden. | Versioned KB-3 market-style extension. | The source supplies choice criteria, not the owner's actual category choice or execution capacity. |
| OA2-06 | 137-144 | Positioning can be tested through a structured sales pitch with qualified best-fit prospects; observe comprehension, excitement, confusion, questions, and deal progress, then decide whether evidence warrants adjustment. | Versioned KB-7 qualified-prospect pitch-validation extension. | A synthetic fixture is not a live pitch. Prospect reactions are evidence, not automatic authority to change positioning. |
| OA2-07 | 145-147 | After live validation, translate positioning into a maintained messaging source before producing channel copy so marketing and sales share one approved story. | Downstream handoff to messaging/copy systems. | The dossier does not authorize writing or approving copy. |
| OA2-08 | 149-151 | Assign a positioning steward, review on material product/market/competitive changes, and run periodic checkpoints; reopen the exercise only when alternatives, distinct capabilities, or value may have shifted. | V3 stewardship and change-control contract. | Review cadence and owner assignment remain company decisions. |

## Source boundary

All eight claims are `use_with_caveat`. A runtime must attach company-specific evidence to every substantive decision and preserve `known`, `inferred`, `unknown`, and `conflicted` states. `SOURCE_GAP` is mandatory whenever a decision depends on book content this dossier does not paraphrase.
