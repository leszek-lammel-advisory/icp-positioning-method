## Claims admitted into KB-4

| Claim ID | Synthesis | Operational consequence |

## Decision method

KB-4 uses Moore as a conditional adoption lens, not a personality labeler. The
agent first asks whether the buyer situation has a discontinuity: a change in
workflow, risk, integration, procurement, or proof burden that makes mere
product availability inadequate. If no such evidence exists, the module must
not manufacture a "chasm". It returns a standard segment hypothesis or asks
for research.

When discontinuity is evidenced, evaluate a proposed initial group in five
parts:

1. **Shared compelling situation:** members face the same urgent job, current
   alternative, trigger, and progress barrier.
2. **Whole-product fit:** integrations, implementation, services, process
   changes, proof, and partner dependencies required for successful use are
   known and have a credible owner.
3. **Reference transfer:** a successful buyer is visible or credible to the
   next buyer because they share an application, language, network, or trusted
   reference channel.
4. **Repeatability:** the offer can repeatedly solve the application without
   hiding bespoke work under a segment label.
5. **Expansion path:** the next adjacent group and the condition that makes the
   reference transferable are stated, not assumed.

## Worked reasoning

An initial group of regulated operations teams may all be reachable on
LinkedIn, but that says nothing about a beachhead. Suppose the evidence instead
shows that each team has the same audit-triggered reconciliation problem, uses
the same manual alternative, needs the same integration before safe use, and
relies on peer references from similar firms. The agent can return a
`beachhead_hypothesis` only if the integration and delivery plan are owned and
the reference link is plausible and evidenced. It must still state that proof
of actual referral or adoption is absent.

If a small vertical contains unrelated workflows, alternatives, and value
chains, it may be convenient targeting but not a coherent beachhead. The
correct decision is `standard_segment_hypothesis` or `research_needed`, not a
made-up adoption story.

## Limits and counterexamples

The source cannot prove somebody is visionary or pragmatic based on job title,
company size, or willingness to buy. Nor can it turn a narrow audience into a
beachhead without evidence of shared conditions and reference transfer. A
whole-product checklist is not a promise that the company can deliver it. KB-5
tests perception, KB-6 diagnoses switching, and actual customer outcomes remain
the authority on adoption.
