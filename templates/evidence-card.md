# Company Evidence Card

Fill this in before you ask for any positioning or ICP decision. The method
only reasons from what is written here. Anything you leave blank stays
`unknown`, and the skill will ask for research instead of guessing.

Give every piece of evidence an ID (E1, E2, ...) so decisions can cite it.

## 2. Customer evidence

Direct evidence beats opinion. Prefer interviews, win/loss notes, call notes,
usage data, and observed workflows.

| ID | Source (who, when, how collected) | Buyer role and situation | What they said or did | Direct or inferred |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 |  |  |  |  |
| E2 |  |  |  |  |

Useful questions to have answers for:

- What changed or broke before they started looking (the struggling moment)?
- What did they do right before buying (spreadsheet, agency, in-house, another tool, nothing)?
- What else did they consider, and what did they rule out?
- What worried them about switching? What habit did they have to give up?
- For losses: what did they choose instead, and why?
